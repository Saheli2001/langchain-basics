We are made of borrowed light,
of fragile bones and stubborn dreams,
walking through ordinary days
as if they might last forever.

We build, we break,
we love, we leave,
and somehow, after every ending,
we learn to begin again.

A human heart is strange—
it can carry yesterday's sorrow
and still make room
for tomorrow's hope.

We reach for the stars
with hands that tremble,
knowing we may never touch them,
yet reaching anyway.

Perhaps that is what makes us human—
not that we are strong,
not that we are perfect,
but that even knowing
how temporary we are,
we continue to love,
to dream,
to create,
and to choose
another morning.
